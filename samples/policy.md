# Example refund policy

This is fictional sample material for trying LocalLens.

## Standard purchases

A customer may request a refund within 30 days of purchase. The original receipt
is required. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method within
5 to 10 business days.

## Exceptions

Custom work is non-refundable once production begins. Subscription renewals
may be refunded within 7 days if no service usage occurred after renewal.

## Support workflow

Ask the customer for the order number and purchase date. Check the applicable
refund window, record the reason, and send the case to the support team.
